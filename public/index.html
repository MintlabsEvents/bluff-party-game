How to Play Bluff Party

1️⃣
Invent a Believable Lie

You get an obscure, true fact with a blank. Write a bluff so convincing that your friends think it's the real truth!

2️⃣
Spot the Truth

All player bluffs and the one Real Truth are shuffled. Vote for the truth! (You can't vote for your own lie).

3️⃣
Rake in the Points!

+1,000 pts for finding the Truth.
+500 pts for every player who voted for your Bluff!
Final Round gives DOUBLE points!
